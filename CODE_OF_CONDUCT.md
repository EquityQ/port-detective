# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive project.
Participants should communicate with empathy, assume good faith, give actionable
feedback, and respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks,
sexualized language or attention, publishing private information, and deliberate
disruption of project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through a private GitHub channel.
Include the relevant link, context, and any evidence that can be shared safely. The
maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where practical, and
may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct that materially affects project
collaboration. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
